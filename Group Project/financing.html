Financial Aid

Students should contact the Financial Aid Office if they have any questions or additional budget items. Additional costs that may be added on a case-by-case basis, such as:

  • Higher than average kits or supplies (program specific)
  • Professional licensure, certification, or a first professional credential (program specific)
  • Federal Direct Student Loan Fees if the student borrows a federal student loan
  • Dependent care
  • Disability-related expenses
  • Travel expenses if the student lives a great distance from campus or needs to travel for their program.

Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA)

The Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) allows you to determine the amount and type(s) of aid you are eligible for. Your financial aid award package will become available after you have applied and been accepted into a financial-aid eligible program.

Scholarships

Scholarships help you pay for college by providing financial assistance that may be applied toward tuition, books, and other school-related expenses.

  • Contact the Financial Aid Office at (608) 757-7664 or btcfinaid@blackhawk.edu with questions.
  • For Blackhawk Foundation Scholarships, please contact the Foundation at (608) 743-4452 or btcfoundation@blackhawk.edu.

Apply for Blackhawk Foundation Scholarships

Each year, more than 300 students receive scholarships through the Blackhawk Foundation. Some scholarships are for applicants in particular fields of study or geographic areas while others are intended to help students overcome personal or financial hardships. Whatever the circumstance, all students are encouraged to apply.

  • Fall scholarship application window is Feb. 1 - April 1
  • Spring scholarship application window is Aug. 15 - Oct. 15

Veterans Benifits

Let us help you find out what benefits you're eligible for and support you as you transition to college life.

Claim your veteran education benefits.

Contact the County Veteran Services Officers (CVSO) for your state regarding eligibility and filling out initial paperwork.

  • County Veteran Services Officers (CVSO) of Wisconsin
  • County Veteran Services Officers (CVSO) of Illinois

After determining your education benefit eligibility with the CVSO, contact Blackhawk's School Certifying Official (SCO) at veterans@blackhawk.edu.

Earn credit for military training and service

As a member of the military, you may also be eligible for college credit based on coursework that you have already completed throughout boot camp, basic training, officer training school, or other training. Please note: Acceptance of credit is at the discretion of the college.

  • Obtain Necessary Documentation: Required documentation is a copy of your DD-214 (Member 4).
  • Submit Documentation: Once documentation is received, Blackhawk staff will review your miliary training and experience documents for awarding of possible credits.
  • Check your Blackhawk Email: All communications regarding any type of Credit for Prior Learning will be sent to your Blackhawk student email address.
